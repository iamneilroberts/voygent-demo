Voygent is an MCP server that rides the user's own AI subscription — the per-session inference is paid by their Claude (or ChatGPT) plan, not by us. The moat is the tools and the orchestration, not a model vendor. Every number below is this demo session's real telemetry.

the defensibility

Model-swappable

A hand-rolled MCP host drives the model — Claude today, ChatGPT or local tomorrow. We don't bet the company on one vendor's pricing.

Tools are the moat

~30 supplier integrations, many edge-native where the industry needs browser farms. Hard to copy, cheap to run.

Two revenue rails

Advisor subscriptions (the pro tool) and affiliate commission on bookings (the consumer front door) — same engine.
